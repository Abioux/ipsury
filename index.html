Career journey

    IPSury Enterprise

  2. Feb 2023 – Feb 2025 · Geneva, Switzerland

    Young Expert, World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)

    Alongside overseeing IPSury, she served at this UN specialised agency as its first Malaysian Young Expert. In her first year she supported Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) users, handled PCT-related matters, published the weekly official PCT Gazette and helped raise IP awareness among youth. In her second year she joined the Customer Experience Section, working on WIPO’s digital content and newsletters.
